# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Teacher**: projects the 上课 page on the classroom screen and runs the lesson (new question → spin → next). Needs to operate it at a glance while talking to the class, often from a distance with a keyboard or clicker.
- **Students**: open the 学生 page on their own phones, pick their name once per lesson, and drag a slider to say how much they want to answer the current question. Interaction lasts seconds per question.

## Product Purpose

A Chinese-language classroom name wheel where students can raise or lower their own chance of being called, using a small credit budget. The teacher only ever sees cumulative stats, never a student's choice on a single question. Success: the wheel is fun to watch, students understand their chance and credits instantly, and nobody has to read instructions.

## Brand Personality

Playful, quick, fair. Feels like a classroom game (Kahoot / Duolingo energy) rather than an admin tool, but never childish or noisy. The spin is the moment of drama; everything else gets out of the way.

## Anti-references

- Wordy UI: helper paragraphs, explanations of rules on every screen, "提示" text under every control.
- Generic admin dashboards: grey cards, tables everywhere.
- Over-decorated "AI" looks: paper/cream textures, heavy black offset shadows on every element.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't explain.** The wheel, the slider and the numbers carry the meaning; text is a label, not a paragraph.
2. **One focal point per screen.** Teacher: the wheel. Student: their slice and their slider.
3. **Readable from the back row.** Projected text and names are big and high-contrast.
4. **Play, not paperwork.** Motion and color make spinning and being picked feel like an event.
5. **Privacy is felt, not lectured.** The UI shows only what each person is allowed to see; no repeated privacy disclaimers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No special requirements beyond WCAG AA contrast and honoring prefers-reduced-motion. Student page must work one-handed on small phones.
