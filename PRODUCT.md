# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Teacher**: projects the 上课 page on the classroom screen and runs the lesson (new question → spin → next). Needs to operate it at a glance while talking to the class, often from a distance with a keyboard or clicker.
- **Students**: open the 学生 page on their own **laptops** (primary) or phones, pick their name once per lesson, and drag a slider to say how much they want to answer the current question. Interaction lasts seconds per question. Laptop layout is two columns (wheel left, chance/slider/result right); phones fall back to one column.

## Product Purpose

A Chinese-language classroom name wheel where students can raise or lower their own chance of being called, using a small credit budget. The teacher only ever sees cumulative stats, never a student's choice on a single question. Success: the wheel is fun to watch, students understand their chance and credits instantly, and nobody has to read instructions.

## Brand Personality

Made by **Silicon Chicken Technologies (硅雞科技)** — company name in Impact next to the logo, top-left only. Color/vibe reference: firechickenproductions.com (the user's other site) — a solid deep-crimson top bar on every page with a thin gold rule. But the UI must feel **light (轻盈)**, not heavy: Impact everywhere felt 笨重, so headings and big numbers use a serif (Noto Serif SC, weights 400–500), body is DM Sans, buttons are thin 1px-outline pills, cards use 1px hairlines, and content is centered. Impact is reserved for the company name only. Simpler colors than that site — only three: deep crimson (oklch 44% 0.13 22), pale champagne gold, and a near-white page tinted toward red. No stripes, orange or green blocks. Text on red is a very pale pink, never pure white (white felt jarring). The home page stays white. Pure black/white/grey reads as funeral / 不吉利 and was rejected; fully saturated red/gold was rejected as ugly.

## Anti-references

- Wordy UI: helper paragraphs, explanations of rules on every screen, "提示" text under every control.
- Toy-like classroom apps: candy colors everywhere, rounded bubbly fonts, emoji.
- Skeuomorphic / metallic finishes: chrome gradients, bevels, glossy highlights (tried and rejected).
- Generic admin dashboards: grey cards, tables everywhere.
- Over-decorated "AI" looks: paper/cream textures, heavy black offset shadows on every element.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't explain.** The wheel, the slider and the numbers carry the meaning; text is a label, not a paragraph.
2. **One focal point per screen.** Teacher: the wheel. Student: their slice and their slider.
3. **Readable from the back row.** Projected text and names are big and high-contrast.
4. **Restraint is the style.** No cards, gradients, or shadows; spacing and hairlines do the structure.
5. **Privacy is felt, not lectured.** The UI shows only what each person is allowed to see; no repeated privacy disclaimers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No special requirements beyond WCAG AA contrast and honoring prefers-reduced-motion. Student page is designed for laptops first and must still work on phones.
